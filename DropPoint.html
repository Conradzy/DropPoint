FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I find water refilling stations near me?

Open the DropPoint app and allow location access. The map automatically shows all verified water refilling stations near you, sorted by distance, with pricing and ratings visible at a glance.

Is DropPoint free for customers?

Yes! DropPoint is completely free to use as a customer. You only pay for the water you order, at the price set by your chosen station. No app subscription or hidden fees.

How does the ₱1 per gallon charge work for station owners?

DropPoint charges station owners ₱1 only when a gallon is successfully delivered to a customer. If an order is cancelled or fails, no fee is charged. It's a performance-based model that rewards completed deliveries.

What is the IoT monitoring feature?

Smart Station and Premium plan subscribers receive an IoT device that connects to their water system. It continuously measures pH, TDS, turbidity, temperature, UV intensity, and flow rate — displaying live readings on their public DropPoint listing so customers can trust the water quality.

How do I register my water station on DropPoint?

Simply sign up as a station owner, submit your business permit and sanitation certificate for verification, and set up your listing. Once approved, your station goes live on the map and customers can immediately start ordering from you.
